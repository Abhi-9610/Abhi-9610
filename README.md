### 🚀 About Me

I'm a software engineer, entrepreneur, and technology enthusiast passionate about building products that solve real-world business problems.

Currently, I'm the **Co-Founder & CTO of BlackMatter Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**, where we're building technology-driven solutions that help businesses simplify operations, automate workflows, and scale efficiently.

My focus is on combining engineering, product thinking, and business understanding to build meaningful software.

- 🏢 **Co-Founder & CTO** at [BlackMatter Technologies](https://blackmattertechnologies.com)
- 💻 Building scalable web applications, SaaS platforms, ERPs & CRMs.
- ✂️ Building **Scissors ERP** – an ERP solution designed for apparel manufacturing businesses.
- ⚙️ Interested in system architecture, backend engineering, automation & AI.
- 🌱 Constantly exploring new technologies and better ways to build software.
- 🎯 Focused on transforming complex business workflows into simple, scalable systems.
- 📍 Based in India.
